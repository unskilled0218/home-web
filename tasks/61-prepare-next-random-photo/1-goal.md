# goal

## command

Display images in chunks of 10. While displaying the current 10 images, choose another 10 images from the photo library without duplicates.

## goal

Play randomly selected batches of 10 images, showing one image at a time. While the current batch plays, select and prepare the next batch. Avoid duplicates within and between batches until every eligible image in the library has been shown, using a smaller batch when fewer than 10 unseen images remain. Repeat with a new traversal after exhausting the library. Refresh the library periodically, and keep the current image visible if preparation fails.
