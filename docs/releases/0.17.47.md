## Changes

Images now matches other dashboard items with separate desktop/mobile visibility and a fullscreen hiding override. The existing **Show image** controls desktop; **Show image on mobile** applies up to and including 600px. **Hide image in fullscreen** temporarily overrides either profile. Choices follow Images Local/Global scope, independently of playback source. Older hidden-image records remain hidden on mobile; explicit choices are preserved across storage, caches, and version-1 imports. Hiding photos still hides blur and filenames without changing playback or widgets. See [dashboard preferences](../dashboard.md) and [settings scope](../settings-scope.md).
